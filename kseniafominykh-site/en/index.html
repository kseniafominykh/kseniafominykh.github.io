About

I work by hand: watercolour, fineliner, coloured pencils, gouache, oil. I also make cut-out and frame-by-frame animation. I want the hand to show in the work.

Before illustration came dance, modelling, my own production company and my own clothing brand — I know every stage of garment making. Now all of it goes into drawing.

Every so often I disappear into a new craft: mosaic, papier-mâché, sewing toys. One day I want to fill my own home with things made by hand, so that it looks like me. And I have an inexplicable pull towards New York.
